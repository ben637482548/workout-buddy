Frequently asked questions

How do I add a workout?

Type its name in the Add New Workout field at the bottom of the screen, then tap the plus button. It stays on your list week after week.

How do I check off a day?

Tap that day's box in the workout's row. Today's box is tinted blue. Tap a box again to uncheck it.

Do the boxes reset every week?

No. Boxes stay checked until you tap them again, so you decide when to clear a week and start fresh. Your history saves each week's boxes, so uncheck last week's boxes when a new week begins.

Where do I see my history?

Tap a workout's name. Below the name and notes you will find a month calendar with a blue dot on every day you checked off. Use the arrows or swipe sideways to change months. History starts the week you first open version 2.0, so earlier months stay empty.

How do I add notes or weights?

Tap a workout's name, type in the notes field, then tap Save. The note shows under the workout's name on the main screen.

How do I reorder or delete workouts?

Tap the ⋯ button at the top right and choose Delete / Reorder, then drag the handles or tap the red buttons. You can also swipe left on a workout and tap Delete. Workout Buddy always asks first, and deleting a workout also deletes its history.

I updated from an older version. Where are my workouts?

They move over automatically the first time you open the update, with your notes and checked boxes. If you ever see "Workouts Need Attention", tap Retry. Your data is not deleted, and if the message stays, please email support.

Is my data synced or backed up?

Your data stays on your iPhone and is not synced between devices. It is included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backups, so restoring a backup brings it back.

What do I need to use Workout Buddy?

An iPhone with iOS 26.5 or later.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

The short version: Workout Buddy does not collect any data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

Who we are

Workout Buddy is an iPhone app made by Benjamin Staab ("we" or "us"). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information we collect

None. Workout Buddy has no accounts and no sign in. It contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third party code that collects data. The app does not send your information to us or to anyone else.

Information stored on your device

The workouts, notes, checked days and history you create are stored only in the app's storage on your iPhone. We cannot see them. Like other app data, they can be included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backups, which Apple and you control under Apple's terms.

Deleting your information

You can delete any workout in the app, which also deletes its history. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone. Copies inside older device backups follow the rules of those backups.

Purchases

Workout Buddy is sold through the App Store. Apple handles the payment, and we never receive your payment details. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

When you contact support

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include. We use it only to reply to you. We do not sell or share it, and we will delete it on request.

Children

Workout Buddy is suitable for all ages and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
